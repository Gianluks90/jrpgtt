# Introduction

---

This is the rulebook for Amulet, a digital board game for 1 to 4 players, where participants take on the role of adventurers exploring a fantasy world, facing challenges, fighting monsters, and completing quests. The following rulebook provides an in-depth understanding of the game mechanics and the automations that make Amulet an engaging and strategic experience.

Select an item from the side menu if you want to learn more about certain elements of the game.
