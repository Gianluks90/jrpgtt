# The Character

---

Every player of Amulet assumes the role of a character within the game world. At the start of the game, the player will be asked to _prepare_ their character, which involves:

- choosing a name;
- choosing an alignment from the three available: Good, Neutral, and Evil;
- distributing 2 attribute points among the three game parameters: Strength, Magic, and Luck.

Once these three elements are determined, the character is ready.

<br>

## Alignment
Alignment does not directly affect the character or the player but does influence the game world. Sometimes an action or event will react to the alignment, producing different effects depending on it.

<br>

## Parameters
A character in Amulet needs only a few parameters to be defined. In addition to those already mentioned, there are two more. Here is a complete list with a brief description of each:

- **HP**: hit points, representing the character's health. If they drop to 0, the character dies, and the player loses the game (or must start over, depending on the agreed rules);
- **MP**: magic points, representing the character's magical energy reserve. They are consumed to cast spells, interact with magical elements in the game, etc. 20% (minimum 1) of MP is regenerated at the end of each turn;
- **Strength**: represents the character's physical strength, important in combat where brute force is measured! Additionally, each point of Strength assigned with a level increase raises the maximum HP by 5%;
- **Magic**: represents the character's magical power, important in combat where magical strength is measured! Additionally, each point of Magic assigned with a level increase raises the maximum MP by 1;
- **Luck**: a special parameter that affects a multitude of situations when a _luck check_ is performed. Each point of Luck increases the result of the luck check by 2 points.
