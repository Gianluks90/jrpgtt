# How to Play

---

Amulet has a simple structure: players take turns moving their character to an adjacent square (orthogonally) and facing whatever the square contains.

## Map
A 10x10 grid hosts the world of Amulet, still to be explored. The map hosts common and special locations. Special locations are listed in the section of the same name in the Rules, while common locations are characterized by one of the possible biomes (Forest, Desert, Plains, Water, Mountain, and Ruins) and a local event (a random event that may or may not persist over time).

> The *Desert* and *Water* biomes are special because they carry a condition that affects the player. At the end of the turn, these conditions take effect: in the Desert, damage is taken if not prepared to face it, while in Water, one can recover strength thanks to its regenerative nature.

It is likely to hear about Regions and Quadrants in various parts of the game, here is a brief explanation of what they are: The map is divided into 4 quadrants composed of 5x5 blocks, they do not have a specific name and are not even so influential on the game, they are for some automations. Each quadrant, however, hosts a special cell always present and always in the same position: the Elemental Sanctuary. The map is also divided into 3 regions (named I, II, and III) which not only represent a real increase in difficulty but also trigger some key moments of the game: the World Event and the revelation of the Final Boss.

## Movement, Rounds, and Turns
The game is played in rounds, each round consists of the turn of all players. During their turn, the player can move their character to an adjacent square (orthogonally) and face whatever the square contains. With the action "End Turn," the turn passes to the next player.

## Local Event
When a new cell is reached (explored or not), if it is not a special location, a local event is triggered. The local event is a random event that can also last over time (a place or someone settles in the cell permanently or temporarily). Once the local event is concluded, it is very likely that the turn will also end.

## World Event
The first time one of the players moves from Region I to Region II, the World Event is triggered. The World Event permanently modifies the map, generally increasing the game's difficulty level. See the appropriate section of the Rules for more details.

## Actions
Everything that can be done translates into _Actions_ that populate the panel in the left column called _Commands_. Depending on the cell the player is in, the contents of the inventory, and other factors, this list may contain more or fewer actions. When an action is disabled (visible but not selectable), it is likely that a usage limit is active (for example, it is not possible to restore HP if they are at maximum). Sometimes actions also show an indication of money or MP required to perform them. If this indication does not show a value, the cost is variable.
